Hyperlexia Arcade20 games • decoding > comprehension • numbers > nonillions

What is hyperlexia? Precocious word-reading — often before age 3 — where decoding runs far ahead of understanding. Hyperlexic kids typically: decode like adults, struggle with meaning, idioms, and inference, excel at letters, patterns, numbers, and symbols, and often absorb second languages from sight alone ("unexpected bilingualism", 38.7% — Gagnon et al. 2026). These 20 games build on those strengths and train the weaker links. Some games mix English + French on purpose.
Built from the literature: Silberberg & Silberberg 1967 • Ostrolenk et al. 2017 (systematic review) • Turkeltaub 2004 (fMRI) • Ostrolenk et al. 2024 (Mol Autism, N=701) • Gagnon et al. 2026 (JCPP) • Macdonald, Luk & Quintin 2021 (ARC).
